Preserve official Indiana DNR permitted wildlife rehabilitator listings; do not add fictional dialable entries.

**Why:** The user chose official DNR contacts for the classroom prototype and asked to avoid invented availability claims.

**How to apply:** Add hours, contact methods, and after-hours instructions only when sourced and dated. Do not infer that a provider is open, closed, accepting an animal, reachable live, or providing 24-hour emergency care from the time of day. Treat recorded guidance as instructions, not live care. Keep scenarios fictional, avoid diagnosis or treatment guidance, and do not instruct untrained capture or handling. If wildlife is already safely contained, do not move or disturb it.
