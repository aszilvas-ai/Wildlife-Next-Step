---
name: Wildlife decision routing
description: Product rules for separating reported safety risks from species/context and unresolved information.
---

Base professional-contact recommendations on reported safety concerns, not animal type, time of day, a parent not being seen, or actions already taken. Before a final route, require meaningful answers for animal type or best guess, injury or urgent concern, movement, immediate danger, and parent/adult animal seen; also retain the weakness/coldness/distress safety check. Blank, unknown, or “not sure” required answers show a “More information needed” result with the unanswered questions marked. Users may continue with uncertainty, but the app must preserve that uncertainty and not suggest a specific path until important details are clear. Uncertainty alone is not a reason to recommend professional contact. Reported risk may route to professional guidance once required answers are clear.

**Why:** The user wants students to be able to continue without pretending they know an answer, while keeping unresolved information from producing a confident route or an automatic contact recommendation.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in rule changes, form questions, demos, and tests. Show a question-specific clue when a user is unsure, keep the answer explicitly uncertain, and provide a clear way to continue to a non-recommendation result. For young wildlife, do not treat a parent not being seen by itself as a reason to recommend contact.
